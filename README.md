# Financial Data Operations & Portfolio Logic Engine (PoC)
🚀 **Live Demo:** [Click here to access the running system](https://investments-demo.netlify.app/#) *(Fully updated with the latest production features, including dynamic English localisation)*

This project is a Proof of Concept (PoC) demonstrating advanced financial data reconciliation, logic auditing, and AI-assisted process automation. Born from the need to solve real-world back-office challenges—such as multi-broker custody tracking, tax compliance, and average cost basis integrity—it has evolved into a robust, automated financial operations engine.

This repository serves as a showcase of my core competencies: translating complex regulatory logic into strict system requirements, ensuring data integrity, and orchestrating AI to implement large-scale mathematical models.

## 🤖 AI Orchestration & Logic QA
Unlike traditional software repositories, the codebase within this project was 100% generated by AI models under my strict strategic direction. My role as the Data Operations Specialist & QA involved:

Prompt Engineering & Context Management: Defining strict logical boundaries and mathematical formulas for the AI to execute.

Console Debugging & Logic Testing: Actively reading browser console logs, isolating broken logic streams, and reverse-engineering complex mathematical bugs to force the AI to produce flawless outputs.

Regulatory Translation: Manually converting over 50 pages of dense, ambiguous tax legislation (Brazilian ReVar) into programmatic rules.

## ⚙️ Back-Office Operations Solved (The Foundation)
1. Multi-Broker Consolidation & Average Cost Basis Integrity
Maintains rigorous mathematical accuracy for the Average Cost Basis across multiple checking and investment accounts. Seamlessly handles custody transfers between different brokers, ensuring historical cost basis and position integrity remain completely intact without data loss.

2. Time-Travel State (Historical Auditing)
Features a robust querying engine capable of reconstructing the exact portfolio position, valuation, and asset distribution at any specific date in the past—crucial for compliance auditing and historical reconciliation.

3. Corporate Actions & 'Data-Com' Entitlements
Automatically processes historical average costs resulting from complex corporate events (e.g., Stock Splits). By inputting corporate action details, the engine queries the historical database to determine exact share quantities held on that specific ex-dividend date, calculating precise entitlements and applying withholding tax deductions automatically.

4. T+2 Settlement & Automated Reconciliation
Incorporates banking holiday calendars to accurately audit T+2 settlement dates for variable income operations. The system actively hunts for "orphan dividends" and reconciles account balances against trade notes, ensuring 100% data integrity without manual intervention.

## 📊 Advanced Analytics & Performance Validation
Mathematical Auditing: Calculates complex profitability algorithms including Internal Rate of Return (IRR), Time-Weighted Return (TWR), and the Extended Money-Weighted Return (XIRR/MWR) to provide an accurate picture of portfolio performance based on irregular cash flows (using the Bisection Method).

Yield Tracking: Differentiates between market yield and the investor's actual Yield on Cost based on historical purchasing prices.

🔄 Dynamic Tax Compliance Engine
Dynamically calculates capital gains and deductions following strict tax rules, seamlessly preparing the audited data for tax filing.

Granular Rules: Strict enforcement of edge-case tax legislations, including logic to handle specific asset bundle exemptions ("Units").

Loss Offsetting: Automatically handles historical loss offsetting and specific withholding tax rules across Day Trade and Swing Trade operations.

## 🛠️ Operational Architecture Notes
Logic Over Frameworks: Built deliberately without heavy frameworks to validate the complex mathematical models and tax rules through strict logic testing.

Hybrid Data Persistence: Integrates Firebase for real-time database management alongside LocalStorage. This ensures reliable cross-device synchronisation while maintaining zero-latency scenario simulations for operations testing.
